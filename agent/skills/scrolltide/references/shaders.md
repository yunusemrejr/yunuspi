- **Sorbet Mesh**: Peach, coral and cream, like melting sorbet

- **Plum Mesh**: Deep plum through rose, dark enough to carry white type

- **Matcha Mesh**: Matcha green against cream and beige
- **Digital Lavender**: Soft periwinkle and lilac with a cool blue lift. Calm, and very of the moment
- **Indigo Mesh**: Indigo and violet in a low key, for dark interfaces
- **Citrus Mesh**: Lemon, lime and a warm orange core. The loudest of the pastels
- **Candy Mesh**: Bubblegum pink, sky blue and lemon. The sweetest thing on the shelf

- **Cobalt Flow**: Deep blue sheets folding through near-black, lit by white blooms where the flow piles up. Heavily dithered, so it reads
- **Ash Flow**: Smoke and silver. The colourless build, for sites that bring their own palette
- **Mesh Drift**: Five soft colour fields drifting through each other, dithered into a print-like surface. Pastel and calm, with no hard edge
- **Ember Wash**: A red mass rolling through white with a black shadow beneath it. Three colours, no detail, all contrast
