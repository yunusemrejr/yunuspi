# Research — web, writing, networking

- Prefer primary sources; rank by authority and recency; cite what you actually opened.
- Search short previews first, open few pages deeply, stop when the question is answered.
- Distinguish observed facts from inference; give dates, units and denominators for numbers.
- Networking: diagnose layer by layer (DNS → route → TLS → HTTP) with bounded probes.
