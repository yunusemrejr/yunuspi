# Resourceful Market Strategy

Use for positioning, customer understanding, messaging and low-resource acquisition experiments. Persuasion should clarify value, not invent urgency or exploit vulnerability.

## Working method

- Identify a concrete audience, need, alternative and buying context.
- Separate observed evidence from assumptions about motives.
- Choose one affordable experiment with an outcome metric.
- Write truthful claims and learn from qualified responses, not vanity counts.

Read [patterns and examples](references/patterns.md) for the relevant implementation mode; do not load unrelated modes. Inspect the actual runtime, project conventions and constraints before choosing syntax, dependencies or deployment steps. User instructions take precedence; this skill adds no authority to change external systems.

## Search-first content surfaces

Start every text surface from what the audience searches for; one clear purpose per page (**one answer, many surfaces**), judged by valuable action, never impressions. See [search-first content surfaces](references/patterns.md#search-first-content-surfaces) for the working method; use [organic growth engineering](../organic-growth-engineering/SKILL.md) for site structure and [search discoverability](../search-discoverability/SKILL.md) to verify visibility.

## Evidence and completion

Use a small representative case and the relevant failure case to check the result. Report what was executed, what remains unverified, and any material compatibility assumption. Do not invent measured outcomes or treat reading this guide as verification.
