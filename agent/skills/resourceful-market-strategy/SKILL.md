# Resourceful Market Strategy

Use for positioning, customer understanding, messaging and low-resource acquisition experiments. Persuasion should clarify value, not invent urgency or exploit vulnerability.

## Working method

- Identify a concrete audience, need, alternative and buying context.
- Separate observed evidence from assumptions about motives.
- Choose one affordable experiment with an outcome metric.
- Write truthful claims and learn from qualified responses, not vanity counts.

Read [patterns and examples](references/patterns.md) for the relevant implementation mode; do not load unrelated modes. Inspect the actual runtime, project conventions and constraints before choosing syntax, dependencies or deployment steps. User instructions take precedence; this skill adds no authority to change external systems.

## Search-first content surfaces

Start every text surface — labels, FAQs, blog posts, titles, tool descriptions, landing pages, About pages, articles — from what the target audience actually searches for. The questions people ask should become the pages that genuinely answer them. Modern discovery is not only backlinks, content volume or traditional SEO: search engines and AI assistants increasingly reward pages that answer the question, cite reliable references, name their sources, explain their reasoning and link useful follow-up material, and AI-assisted content must still show whose knowledge, research or expertise it rests on.

Give each page one clear purpose and connect it to related articles, tools, solutions, references and the logical next step, with the same promise kept across articles, FAQs, tool descriptions and landing pages. The principle is **one answer, many surfaces**: answer the same question at different levels across the site, because the site — not the set of channels you cannot cover heavily — is the durable asset. Optimize for humans and for Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yandex, ChatGPT, Gemini and other discovery systems at once, but judge the result by valuable action (right people, right pages, what they came for, earned trust, the next useful step), never by impressions alone.

See [search-first content surfaces](references/patterns.md#search-first-content-surfaces) for the working method; use [organic growth engineering](../organic-growth-engineering/SKILL.md) for site structure, internal links and machine-readable publishing, and [search discoverability](../search-discoverability/SKILL.md) to verify crawlability, metadata and AI-answer visibility.

## Evidence and completion

Use a small representative case and the relevant failure case to check the result. Report what was executed, what remains unverified, and any material compatibility assumption. Do not invent measured outcomes or treat reading this guide as verification.
